\section{A complete direct inverse model on fourfold \(\mathrm{Sp}_4\)}
\label{sec:sp4-direct-inverse}

Gao's Theorem~6.5 \cite{GaoInverseEis} supplies a global alternative to
the three-theta quartic product. It assumes \(K\supset\mu_8\), the
specified fourfold cover of \(\mathrm{Sp}_4\), an exceptional automorphic
character \(\chi\) on the Siegel Levi center, and a finite set \(S\)
containing all ramified places and places above \(2\). The Levi theta
representation is distinguished. The source proves
\[
 E_\psi(1,f_s,\Theta(\chi))
 =\frac{B_\chi(s)}{L_K^S(4s+3,\chi_{\alpha_2})},
 \qquad B_\chi(s)=\prod_{v\in S}W_{f_{s,v}}(1).
\]
This global factorization is an external input. The two rational
identities below are kernel checked; no adelic or automorphic
formalization is claimed for them.

\paragraph{Normalize the actual \(L\)-variable first.}
For unitary central inducing data, the real torus exponents satisfy
\(2(\lambda_1-\lambda_2)=1\) and \(\lambda_1+\lambda_2=0\), hence
\((\lambda_1,\lambda_2)=(1/4,-1/4)\). Since \(n_{\alpha_2}=4\),
write \(\chi_{\alpha_2}=\eta|\cdot|^{-1}\) with \(\eta\) unitary. Thus
\[
 u=4s+2,\qquad E_\psi(1,f_s,\Theta(\chi))
                 =B_\chi(s)/L_K^S(u,\eta).
\]
The character-image adapter is separate. A determinant twist by \(\tau\)
changes \(\eta\) to \(\eta\tau^4\); the source does not assert that every
desired character occurs. A zeta application must exhibit \(\eta=1\),
or a proved packet containing the Riemann-zeta factor.

\paragraph{Keep the complete Weyl sum and scattering quotient.}
Set \(q=Nv\) and \(x=\chi'_s(\overline h_{\alpha_2}(\varpi^4))\), where
\(\chi'=w_M\chi\). Source relation (46) gives coordinates \(x,qx,q^2x\).
The four surviving cells on pages 512--514 are
\[
\begin{split}
 A&=\frac{1-x/q}{1-q^2x},\\
 B&=\frac{1-x/q}{1-qx}\frac{1-q^{-1}}{1-(q^2x)^{-1}},\\
 C&=\frac{1-x/q}{1-x}\frac{1-q^{-1}}{1-(qx)^{-1}},\\
 D&=\frac{1-q^{-1}}{1-(qx)^{-1}}
       \frac{1-q^{-1}}{1-x^{-1}}
       +q^{-1}\frac{1-x}{1-(qx)^{-1}}.
\end{split}
\]
In particular the last denominator in \(D\) contains the inverse.
The complete identity \(A+B+C+D=1-x/q\) is maintained as
\texttt{Metaplectic.Sp4FourInverse.whittaker\_weyl\_sum},
over any field with \(q,x\ne0\) and
\(1-x,1-qx,1-q^2x\ne0\). Continuation to singular coordinates belongs
to the source, not to division in this field identity.

The independent three-root scattering product is
\[
 \frac{1-x/q}{1-x}\frac{1-x}{1-qx}\frac{1-qx}{1-q^2x}
 =\frac{1-x/q}{1-q^2x}.
\]
This is maintained as
\texttt{Metaplectic.Sp4FourInverse.scattering\_telescope}.
It needs only \(1-x,1-qx\ne0\), retaining the last denominator.
Now \(x=\eta(v)q^{-4s-1}\). Its product, initially for \(\Re u>4\), is
\[
 c^S(s)=\frac{L_K^S(u-3,\eta)}{L_K^S(u,\eta)}.
\]
This is the unramified scalar. Every ramified intertwining operator,
archimedean factor, section norm and moving parameter remains in the
full global transformation.

\paragraph{Carry the model to its actual exponent consumer.}
A hypothetical zero \(\rho\) is read at
\[
 s_\rho=(\rho-2)/4,\qquad
 7/8\longleftrightarrow-9/32,\qquad
 3/4\longleftrightarrow-5/16.
\]
Detection also requires local data with \(B_\chi\) holomorphic and
nonzero at \(s_\rho\). Neither that choice nor its moving-family norms
are discharged by quoting the factorization.

For \(w\in C_c^\infty(0,\infty)\), the inverse arithmetic observable is
\[
 A_\eta(X;w)=
 \sum_{\substack{\mathfrak a\ne0\\(\mathfrak a,S)=1}}
 \mu_K(\mathfrak a)\eta(\mathfrak a)w(N\mathfrak a/X)
 =\frac1{2\pi i}\int_{\Re z=c}
       \frac{\widehat w(z)X^z}{L_K^S(z,\eta)}\,dz,\quad c>1.
\]
An independent bound \(A_\eta(X;w)\ll_{\eta,w,\epsilon}
X^{3/4+\epsilon}\) for all \(X\ge2\), with windows detecting every
hypothetical zero, would prove the corresponding native boundary.
There is no quartic reference convolution in this observable.

The logarithmic detector is also available. Put
\(L_\eta(s)=L_K^S(4s+2,\eta)\). Its exact operator is
\[
 L_\eta(s)E_\psi'(s)-B_\chi'(s)
 =-4B_\chi(s)\frac{(L_K^S)'(4s+2,\eta)}{L_K^S(4s+2,\eta)}.
\]
Both \(B_\chi'\) and the chain factor \(4\) are compulsory.
Multiplication by \(L_\eta\) means Mellin convolution before the common
physical cutoff. The resulting prime-power sum keeps its principal
subtraction, all higher prime powers and finite \(S\)-corrections.

This comparison removes two global adapters: the quartic reference
factor and completion of a three-theta product. It does not transfer
the cubic \(3/16\) moment exponent or a quadratic-prefix norm to the
new observable. The actual character image, local nonvanishing,
physical transformation and independent signed estimate must still
match. Spectral reflection sends \(u\) to \(4-u\), not \(1-u\);
the unitary axis is \(\Re u=2\). Unitarity there does not exclude poles
in the negative spectral strip. Dropping the reciprocal in \(c^S\)
would discard the zero signal.

The benchmark is unchanged: old bound \(7/8\), new bound \(7/8\).
The two algebraic proofs check a complete source calculation; they
supply no independent \(3/4\) estimate and carry no novelty claim for
Gao's construction.
