\section{Where the quadratic labels disappear, and an amplifier preflight}
\label{sec:sp4-amplifier-preflight}

This section records a failed transfer of an existing bound, before
building a new helper pipeline. It identifies the signed expressions
that such a pipeline would actually have to estimate. It is a
source-dependent analysis, not an additional Lean theorem or a new
zero-free result.

\paragraph{Full local integration leaves power resonances.}
At a good place write \(q\) for the residue cardinality. The completed
local spherical Jacquet integral is exactly
\[
 1-q^{-4s-2}=1-q^{-2}T^4,\qquad T=q^{-s}.
\]
All Weyl terms and all geometric valuation strata have been included
before this equality is used. If the absolutely convergent initial
Jacquet integral is grouped by its integral flat-section height \(h\),
write its coefficients as \(a_h\). An unramified determinant twist
\(\tau\) inserts \(\tau(\varpi)^h\) at that height. Uniqueness of
the resulting convergent power series gives
\[
 a_0=1,\qquad a_4=-q^{-2},\qquad a_h=0\quad(h\ne0,4).
\]
For a quartic twist the surviving phases are
\(\tau(\varpi)^0=\tau(\varpi)^4=1\).
In terms of the quadratic label \(\kappa=\tau^2\), both surviving
half-height classes are squares. Applying a quadratic-character sieve
\emph{after} this exact integration therefore averages invariant
columns. The nontrivial label alone gives no power saving there.

This says nothing of the signs of individual geometric summands:
the vanishing coefficients are signed integrals of entire strata.
It does not rule out a better estimate of the full geometric expression
\emph{before} integration, with suitable ramified data and uniform
norms. In particular it is not an impossibility theorem for the
original coupled packet. It pinpoints why the ordinary quadratic
matrix cannot be inserted into the already collapsed Euler factor.

\paragraph{A genuine character-dependent Hecke observable.}
The metaplectic Satake theorem \cite{McNamaraSatake} supplies a spherical
Hecke operator whose transform is the orbit sum
\[
 z_1+z_2+z_1^{-1}+z_2^{-1}
\]
on the square-coordinate lattice \(Y_{Q,4}=2\mathbb Z^2\).
Here the modified dual roots are \(e_1-e_2,2e_2\).
The anti-exceptional principal data, with the determinant twist, give
\[
 z_1=\kappa(\varpi)q^{1/2-2s},\qquad
 z_2=\kappa(\varpi)q^{-1/2-2s}.
\]
Consequently its eigenvalue is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:sp4-character-hecke-eigenvalue}
 \lambda_{\kappa,q}(s)=\kappa(\varpi)L_q(s),\qquad
 L_q(s)=(q^{1/2}+q^{-1/2})(q^{2s}+q^{-2s}).
\end{equation}
The Hecke operator here is defined by its \emph{full inverse Satake
transform}. Replacing it by an uncomputed raw Cartan double coset
would discard possible lower terms and is not licensed by this
calculation. No bound for its geometric kernel is asserted.

Choose a finite set \(\mathcal P\) of rational primes splitting
completely in \(K\), outside the bad set, and one place above each.
Write \(\ell\) for their norms. For a fixed candidate zero
\(\rho=\beta+i\gamma\), set \(s_\rho=(\rho-2)/4\) and take the
amplifier coefficients
\(\overline{\lambda_{\kappa,\ell}(s_\rho)}\).
Its exact eigenvalue is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:sp4-full-hecke-amplifier}
 A_{\mathcal P,\rho}(s)
 =\sum_{\ell\in\mathcal P}\overline{L_\ell(s_\rho)}L_\ell(s).
\end{equation}
The two quadratic character phases multiply to one. Thus the
amplified completed period is still exactly
\(A_{\mathcal P,\rho}(s)B(s)/\zeta_{\mathbb Q}^{(2)}(4s+2)\).
All companion and ramified-restoration factors from
Section~\ref{sec:sp4-fixed-spectrum} remain present.

For fixed finite \(\mathcal P\) the multiplier at the zero is
\(A_{\mathcal P,\rho}(s_\rho)=\sum|L_\ell(s_\rho)|^2>0\).
Indeed \(\Re s_\rho<0\) and the reverse triangle inequality gives
\[
 |L_\ell(s_\rho)|^2
 \ge \ell^{3-\beta}(1-\ell^{\beta-2})^2.
\]
For \(3/4\le\beta\le7/8\) and \(\ell\ge17\) the constant
\((1-17^{-9/8})^2\) is a uniform lower factor. Thus a nonempty
amplifier preserves the pole with its order. Passing to
\(\mathcal P=\mathcal P(X)\) additionally needs the appropriate
uniform detector and window estimates; fixed-parameter pole
nonvanishing alone is not that transfer.

\paragraph{The complete physical dilation identity.}
Let \(\mathcal M_B(X)\) be the inverse Mellin wave with integrand
\[
 \frac{B((u-2)/4)\widehat w(u)}{\zeta_{\mathbb Q}^{(2)}(u)}X^u.
\]
One can first use finite vertical segments, so no infinite-contour
claim is needed for the following finite-sum substitution. Define
\[
 b_\ell(\rho)=(\ell+2+\ell^{-1})
       (\ell^{(\bar\rho-2)/2}+\ell^{(2-\bar\rho)/2}).
\]
The full amplified wave is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:sp4-signed-prime-dilation-packet}
 \mathcal A_{B,\rho}(X)=
 \sum_{\ell\in\mathcal P} b_\ell(\rho)
 \left\{\ell^{-1}\mathcal M_B(X\sqrt\ell)
                  +\ell\mathcal M_B(X/\sqrt\ell)\right\}.
\end{equation}
This follows from \(\ell^{2s}=\ell^{-1}\ell^{u/2}\) and
\(\ell^{-2s}=\ell\ell^{-u/2}\). Both dilations and both
conjugate phase terms are retained. The formula is not a sum of
independent window errors.

Here is the full quantitative preflight. Suppose the already available
input for this fixed admissible Mellin window is
\(\mathcal M_B(U)\ll U^\theta\), \(U\ge1\), with
\(1/2\le\theta\le1\). Take \(\ell\in[P,2P]\), \(P\ge17\),
and \(P\le X^{2-\eta}\), for a fixed \(\eta>0\). Both dilation
arguments then grow. Inserting only that input into the two retained
terms gives the fallback envelope
\begin{align*}
 |\mathcal A_{B,\rho}(X)|
 &\ll X^\theta |\mathcal P|
   \left(P^{1+(\theta-\beta)/2}
                 +P^{3-(\beta+\theta)/2}\right)\\
 &\ll X^\theta |\mathcal P|P^{3-(\beta+\theta)/2}.
\end{align*}
This envelope is a test of the \emph{existing-input transfer}, not
a new estimate of the signed main packet. The second term dominates
by the factor \(P^{2-\theta}\). The formal zero-response coefficient
has size at least
\(c|\mathcal P|P^{3-\beta}X^\beta\). Its power ratio to this
envelope is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:sp4-hecke-no-free-boundary-change}
 X^{\beta-\theta}P^{(\theta-\beta)/2}
       =(X/\sqrt P)^{\beta-\theta}.
\end{equation}
Thus feeding in \(\theta=7/8+\epsilon\), where the fixed-window
transfer is justified, does not improve that exponent. An independent
joint estimate of \eqref{eq:sp4-signed-prime-dilation-packet} is still
required. Taking \(P\) beyond \(X^2\) is not a remedy: the shorter
window no longer tends to infinity, and the above bound and moving
zero-response transfer cannot then be reused.

More precisely, if a new estimate saves \(X^\Delta\) against this
envelope and \(P=X^\xi\), \(0\le\xi<2\), the power comparison
requires
\[
 \Delta>(\theta-\beta)(1-\xi/2).
\]
At \(\theta=7/8\), target \(\beta=3/4\), and \(P=X\), this is
\(\Delta>1/16\) in \(X\). The actual shorter scale is
\(U=X/\sqrt P=X^{1/2}\), so this is the same \(1/8\) improvement
in \(U\); the rescaling has not supplied it. Every growing-height,
Hecke-kernel and moving-window loss must be added to this comparison.

\paragraph{What remains open after the preflight.}
The permitted next estimate concerns the complete signed packet
\eqref{eq:sp4-signed-prime-dilation-packet}, or its full geometric
realization before applying the eigenvalue identity. The existing
quadratic sieve has not yet been matched to that realization with
row-independent coefficients. The spherical collapsed factor is
invariant, and inserting an already known bound into the Hecke
identity merely transfers that same bound. These are failures of
two specified transfers, not a proof that the arithmetic programme
is exhausted. No additional theorem is counted as Lean checked;
the adelic Satake and Jacquet interfaces remain source-dependent.
The certified global boundary remains \(7/8\).
